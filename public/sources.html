What we could not verify

The calculator shows "rate on request" for these instead of guessing:

  • UBL CashPlus, Standard Chartered Personal Loan, Faysal Islami Personal Finance, Meezan Consumer Ease, Standard Chartered Saadiq — rates not published on the banks' own pages; secondary figures conflict.
  • NBP Advance Salary — the figures conflict: 23.5% (permanent) vs 25.5% (contractual) vs KIBOR + 9–12% (aggregators).
  • Bank of Punjab Advance Salary — 34.99% a year is quoted by secondary sites only; no official product page was found.
  • PalmPay, NeemPaymenow, Nova Finance — no published price or fee schedule.
  • Khushhali, U Bank, NRSP, FINCA personal loans — no current pricing found on official channels; only old aggregator ranges.
  • Per-app terms for the 17 SECP nano apps — no public rate, limit or late-fee schedule exists; terms are in-app only (Borrower Factsheet).
  • Whether the SECP nano caps survived the Nov 2025 amendments (SRO 2120(I)/2025) — unconfirmed.
  • Late fees for most bank personal loans — they sit in each bank's schedule of charges, not on the product pages, and were not extracted for UBL, MCB, Alfalah, SCB, NBP, BOP, JS, Allied, First Women Bank, Soneri, Silkbank or Bank Al Habib.
  • Easypaisa's Oct–Dec 2026 schedule — verified only via Pakera's reading (19 Oct 2026), not by opening the current PDF directly.
  • Abhi's current fee and licensing status — absent from the Sep 2026 whitelist but still operating; its flat fee (Rs 100 vs 150) is unresolved.
  • QisstPay "comeback" (Profit magazine, Apr 2026) — unverified; no live product found.
  • PACRA ~139% annualised yield on Easypaisa's loan book (2025) — from the research brief, not independently re-verified.

Who lends the money?

Easycash, Easypaisa BNPL and Daraz PayLater are from Easypaisa Bank (Telenor Microfinance Bank). ReadyCash and JazzCash BNPL are from Mobilink Microfinance Bank. Both are microfinance banks regulated by the State Bank of Pakistan — they do not appear on the SECP digital-lending-app whitelist, because the SBP regulates them, not the SECP.

PACRA put the annualised yield on Easypaisa's loan book at about 139% in 2025 (from the research brief — not independently re-verified). That is why the calculator shows the true cost plainly instead of hiding it behind "5% a week".
